Usability and Performance Evaluation of OmniSense

Hello, I am a final-year student at Laksamana College of Business. As part of my final year project, I developed OmniSense, an offline Android app that uses on-device AI to help people who have hearing impairments and hard-of-hearing users with speech-to-text, sound/hazard recognition, and smartwatch vibration alerts. This survey takes about 5-8 minutes to complete and is designed to gather your honest feedback on the interactivity, ease of use and performance of the app. All responses are completely anonymous and will be used strictly for educational purposes and project evaluation. Thank you so much for your time and help.

What to Expect:
The survey consists of 20 questions and will take approximately 5 to 8 minutes to complete.

  • There are no right or wrong answers, honest feedback regarding bugs, false alarms, and performance issues is vital to the research findings.
  • All data collected will be used solely for academic research, analysis, and reporting within the scope of this academic project.
  • Your responses will remain completely anonymous and confidential. Other than asking for your age (for demographic purposes), no personal identifying details like your name, email, or Google account will be collected or published.
  • Your participation is entirely voluntary. You may choose to skip questions or withdraw from the study at any time by closing the browser window without penalty.
